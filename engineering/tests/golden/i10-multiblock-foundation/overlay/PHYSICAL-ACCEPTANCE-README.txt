I10 physical multiplayer acceptance package.

Windows: double-click START-I10-MULTIPLAYER.bat
Linux/macOS: run ./START-I10-MULTIPLAYER.sh

The launcher starts the dedicated server, Client A, and Client B, prepares the canonical UNFORMED baseline, connects both real clients, and records complete logs under build/i10-multiplayer-acceptance/.

Physical formation and visual observations remain required by the acceptance protocol. The wrappers do not manufacture screenshots, player interaction, reconnect evidence, or PASS state.
